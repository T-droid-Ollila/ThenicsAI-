<p align="center">
  <b>AI-Powered Exercise Recognition & Pose Estimation</b>
</p>

- **Take the following use case , you are a rookie in Calisthenics,you don't have personal trainer or don't have the funds to access one , you training consists of watching youtube videos and hopefully implementing the right but no one is right there to track your from in real time , your workout also consists of inconsistent training schedules whereby you can train on Monday , plan to train on Wednesday but your not in the mood , the end result is a lack of proper tracking mechanism to ensure that gains missed on those days were recovered on a later date, Enter THENICS AI.** 

- **ThenicsAI is an AI powered exercise recognition app built with the most powerful technologies in the AI era. This product analyses a seuence of moves one at a time in real time and identifies whether the moves done are in the right form through a computer vision model .It analyses how the moves the were done wrong and right by checking posture,angular movement..The app also makes use of a recommender system powered by companies such as Spotify to give the recommended reps , schedule based on past performance of user history , fitness levels, goals and available equipment. A Computer vision model which enables real time form tracking based on posture,angular movement to ensure you don't waste time hitting those gains**

- **Implementing this technology will be made easy through the use of [RAY](https://www.anyscale.com/product/open-source/ray) which enables us to do distributed computing , productionizing and scaling thanks to remote clusters(GPU'S) made available to us through the platform.In this project we shall use the best open source tools detailed in the README in the Github project repository.**
