<p align="center">
  <b>AI-Powered Exercise Recognition & Pose Estimation</b>
</p>

- **Take the following use case , you are a rookie in Calisthenics,you don't have personal trainer or don't have the funds to access one , you training consists of watching youtube videos and hopefully implementing the right but no one is right there to track your from in real time enter Thenics AI.**

- **ThenicsAI is an AI powered exercise recognition app built with the most powerful technologies in the AI era. This prodcut analyses a seuence of moves one at a time in real tine and identifies whether the moves done are in the right form.It analyses how the moves the were done wrong and right by checking posture,angular movement**

- **Implementing this technology will be made easy through the use of RAY which enables us to do distributed computing , productionizing and scaling thanks to remote clusters(GPU'S) made available to us through the platform.In this project we shall use the best open source tools detailed in the README in the Github project repository.**
